# Per-candidate experimental plans

Generated by `scripts/32_assay_designs_doc.py` from the tables in `results/tables/`. Every sequence, size and count below is read from those files; none is typed by hand.

## How to read an entry

**Switch** names the two transcripts whose ratio changes. The candidate is the isoform whose fraction moves with LPS; the partner is the most-opposed isoform at the same locus. A positive dIF means the candidate's share of the locus RISES on LPS.

**Platform** is the question this project exists to keep straight: whether a short-read Illumina experiment against the standard annotation could have found this switch, or whether it is visible only because of the PacBio assembly. Two situations require long reads: either side having a non-matching class code, and -- less obvious -- both isoforms mapping to the SAME reference transcript, which makes the switch invisible to any isoform-level short-read analysis no matter how deeply sequenced.

**Expected readout** gives the predicted product size in BOTH isoforms. Where the partner column says *no product*, the assay is isoform-specific by construction; where both sizes are given, one reaction reports the ratio and the two bands are the readout.

**Off-target loci** is measured, not predicted: every oligo was searched against all 296,613 transcripts of the reference by its 3'-terminal 15 nt, then extended to its full length. Binding several isoforms of the same gene is expected and often intended. Binding a *different* gene is a defect, and is stated per oligo.

## Before you order: three caveats that apply to the whole list

1. **The reference FASTA is not uniformly spliced cDNA.** 36,115 of its 296,613 records (12.2%) are unspliced genomic intervals. 14 of the 97 sequences needed here were among them and were rebuilt from hg38 using the GFF3 exon coordinates, a method validated by reproducing four already-correct transcripts base-for-base on both strands. Primers below are designed on verified spliced sequence. Anything else in the project that read sequence or ORFs from that FASTA should be re-checked.

2. **These oligos are designed and screened, not tested.** The specificity numbers are exact-match counts against a transcriptome, which is a far better check than a thermodynamic score but is not a gel. Every pair still needs its standard curve and melt curve.

3. **Antibody and mass-spectrometry routes are specified, not validated.** Where an entry calls for an isoform-resolving western, the epitope requirement is stated so a human can check whether such an antibody exists. I cannot validate antibodies.
